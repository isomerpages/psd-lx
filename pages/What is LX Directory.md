<p>The Leadership Xchange Directory is an&nbsp;informal support network for
leaders to offer advice and share their leadership experiences with others.&nbsp;Anyone,
young officers or established leaders, can reach out for advice and support.
As this is not part of a formal&nbsp;programme,&nbsp;it is really up to
officers to make the best use of the network.</p>
<h4><em>Be part of the Leadership Xchange today!</em></h4>
<p>Simply submit a profile of yourself and list areas that you would like
to share about <a href="https://go.gov.sg/leadershipxchangesignup" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank"><u>here</u></a>.
This could be how you have&nbsp;managed an organisation in crisis, how
you have&nbsp;charted a path for a unit undergoing transformation, or how
you have&nbsp;dealt with personal challenges and opportunities over the
years.</p>
